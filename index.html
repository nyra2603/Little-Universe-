03 / LITTLE CONSTELLATIONS

A few pages from the middle

Not texts and dates...
Little pieces of us.

Tap a memory
to unfold it ↘

The Midnight Library became more than a book on a reading list. We became each other's buddy readers, sharing thoughts about all the different lives a story can hold — and all the ways a person can keep moving towards a better ending.

BOOKS · POSSIBILITY · HOPE

Somewhere along the way, even an ordinary rainy day could turn into an entire imagined scene — the kind with a rain dance, laughter, and a world that briefly feels like a film.

RAIN · IMAGINATION · PLAY

Sanitizer, accessories, books and stationery, a crisp bed, notebooks — the tiny details that make someone feel less like a name on a screen and more like a whole person with their own wonderfully specific universe.

SMALL DETAILS · EVERYDAY MAGIC

A reminder that happiness has its own soundtrack: different voices, spontaneous dancing, and those moments when the excitement is too big to stay quiet.

VOICES · DANCING · JOY

Travel stories, bus rides, the no-earphones rule, and the familiar sequence of getting home, taking a shower, and finally being able to sleep. I started collecting these details without even realising it.

ROUTINES · STORIES · DISTANCE

And there are so many more little moments that won't fit on one page...
